Prática de conversação — verbos irregulares no pretérito perfeito

Speaking practice with 14 irregular verbs. Each question pushes you to use a different personal pronoun.
Pretérito perfeito Verbos irregulares B1 · Conversação
How to use this page. Look at the photo for each verb, read the questions and answer in complete sentences, out loud in class or in writing here. Notice the pronoun in each question: você asks about you (answer with eu), vocês asks about a group that includes you (answer with nós or a gente), and questions about other people need ele, ela, eles or elas.
