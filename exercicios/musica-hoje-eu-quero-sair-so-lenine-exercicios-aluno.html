Hoje Eu Quero Sair Só

Lenine · from the album O Dia em que Faremos Contato, 1997 · 5min38 · composed by Lenine, Mu Chebabi, and Caxa Aragão

A gentle request, not a breakup: the singer asks for one evening alone — no one following him, no one locking anything up — and promises to be back soon. It's a great excuse to study one of the most useful patterns in Portuguese: a conjugated verb followed by an infinitive, the structure behind wanting, needing, and being able to do something.

Grammar

Verbo + infinitivo

Many sentences are built on one pattern: a conjugated verb + a second verb in the infinitive. The first verb carries the person and the tense; the second never changes. This is how Portuguese expresses what you want, what you need, and what isn't possible.

EstruturaFunçãoExemplo
querer + infinitivovontade / wantingHoje eu quero ficar em casa
ter que + infinitivoobrigação / obligationEu tenho que trabalhar amanhã
poder + infinitivopossibilidade / possibilityEu não posso sair hoje
ir + infinitivofuturo próximo / near futureEu vou voltar logo

Only the first verb changes:

euquero sairtenho que sairposso sair

nósqueremos sairtemos que sairpodemos sair

Two more from the song

dever + infinitivo — here it's not obligation, it's probability. Ele deve estar na esquina = he's probably at the corner.
Careful: unlike English "should", dever + infinitive usually means "probably".

deixar + infinitivo — giving permission. Me deixa sair = let me go out.

Cultural context

🎶 Lenine e o Recife
Lenine was born in Recife in 1959 and is one of the most original composers in Brazilian music of the last few decades. His work blends the percussion and rhythms of the Northeast with pop, rock, and electronic production — a combination that gained momentum in the Pernambuco music scene of the 1990s. "Hoje Eu Quero Sair Só" came out on the album O Dia em que Faremos Contato, 1997, and became one of his best-known songs. The lyrics aren't about ending a relationship: they're about remaining a whole person within one — going out alone tonight, coming back soon, and that not being a threat to anyone.
