Language Reference

Possessive Pronouns in Brazilian Portuguese

Possessive pronouns show who something belongs to — my, your, our. In Portuguese, they agree with the thing owned (gender and number of the noun) — not with the owner. This is different from English!

OwnerMasculineFeminine
eu (my) meu / meus minha / minhas
você / ele / ela (your / his / her) seu / seus sua / suas
nós (our) nosso / nossos nossa / nossas
vocês / eles / elas (your (pl) / their) seu / seus sua / suas
▶ Agreement is with the object, not the owner. Ela is feminine, but if she owns a carro (masculine), you say seu carro — not "sua carro."
▶ Seu/sua can be ambiguous — it can mean "your," "his," "her," or "their." When it's unclear, Brazilians often say o carro dele (his car), a casa dela (her house), or o cachorro deles (their dog) instead.
▶ Meu amor, minha filha… — possessives are also used in affectionate terms of address, very common in everyday speech.
Atenção · False Friend

"Nossa!" is not always possessive

Confusingly, the exact same word "Nossa" is also one of the most common exclamations in Brazilian Portuguese — and in that case, it has nothing to do with possession.

Nossa! is short for "Nossa Senhora!" (Our Lady! — a religious expression), but today it's used the way English speakers use "Wow!", "Oh my gosh!" or "No way!" — to show surprise, admiration, or shock. It's used constantly, in almost every conversation, and doesn't need a noun after it.
Possessivo (our)

Nossa casa é grande. (Our house is big.)

Interjeição (wow!)

Nossa! Que casa grande! (Wow! What a big house!)

👉 Tip: if "Nossa" is followed directly by a noun (nossa casa, nosso carro), it's possessive. If it stands alone or comes before an exclamation, it's the interjection — like "Wow!"

Exercise 4

Free production

Write 5 sentences in Portuguese about your life — your family, your things, your city. Use at least 4 different possessives (meu, minha, seu, sua, nosso, nossa). In your last sentence, use "Nossa!" as an exclamation (not a possessive)!

Meu/Minha ___ é ___.  ·  Este é o meu ___.  ·  Esta é a minha ___.
Nossa cidade é ___.  ·  Nosso ___ está em ___.
