Tão ou tanto?

Two small words that add intensity. Tão = so (+ adjective or adverb). Tanto, tanta, tantos, tantas = so much, so many (+ noun, or after a verb). Let's clear it up!
tão = so tanto = so much tanta · tantos · tantas expressões com tanto

Language Reference

Tão or tanto? Start here

Both words intensify something, but they work with different types of words. Look at the word that comes next and choose:

WordUsed withEnglish
tãoadjective or adverbso
tantoverb (after it)so much
tanto, tanta, tantos, tantasnoun (before it)so much, so many
Don't worry if adjective, adverb and noun are not your favorite words. Just focus on the examples and their translations.
1tão = so — with adjectives and adverbs
João é tão alto!John is so tall!
Este café está tão bom!This coffee is so good!
Ana fala tão rápido!Ana speaks so fast!
Eu acordei tão cedo hoje.I woke up so early today.
Tão never changes and always comes before the word it modifies. It is an adverb of intensity.
2tanto = so much — with verbs
Ana fala tanto.Ana talks so much.
Joana estuda tanto!Joana studies so much!
Léo comeu tanto na festa!Léo ate so much at the party!
Eu ri tanto que minha barriga doeu.I laughed so much that my belly hurt.
With verbs, tanto never changes (no tanta, tantos, tantas) and usually comes after the verb. You can add que to say the result: tanto que…
3tanto, tanta, tantos, tantas — with nouns

Before a noun, tanto agrees in gender and number with it. Use the singular for things you can't count (so much) and the plural for things you can count (so many).

singular (so much)plural (so many)
masculinetanto açúcartantos livros
femininetanta paciênciatantas roupas
Eu coloquei tanto açúcar no café.so much sugar
Carina tem tanta paciência com todo mundo.so much patience
Clara tem tantos livros.so many books
Maria tem tantas roupas.so many clothes
4Same idea, different word: tão × tanto
tanto + noun / verbtão + adjective / adverb
Mariana tem tanta sorte.
so much luck		Mariana é tão sortuda.
so lucky
Tiago tem tanto talento.
so much talent		Tiago é tão talentoso.
so talented
Ana fala tanto.
talks so much		Ana fala tão alto.
talks so loudly
5Useful expressions with tanto recognize
Tem tanta gente aqui!There are so many people here!
Ela tem trinta e tantos anos.She is thirty-something (probably 35+).
Quarenta e tantas pessoas vieram.Over forty people came.
Lá pelas tantas, as pessoas começaram a ir embora.At some point (late), people started to leave.
Eu tomei um susto e tanto.I got quite a scare.
Foi uma festa e tanto!It was a great party!
Number + e tanto(s): trinta e tantos = a bit more than thirty. Noun + e tanto: uma festa e tanto = a really great (or big) one.

Part 1 — Compreensão

Understand the text

Read the conversation and answer in Portuguese.

1. Como foi a festa da Marina? (How was Marina's party?)

2. O que o Léo comeu muito? (What did Léo eat a lot of?)

3. Quem cantou na festa? Como ele canta? (Who sang? How does he sing?)
