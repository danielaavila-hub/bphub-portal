Materials Showcase

Brazilian Portuguese Hub · a sample of the original materials used in private lessons

The activities below showcase our materials portal. Each one was designed to support one-on-one Brazilian Portuguese lessons and reflects the approach used throughout our portal.

The activities include comprehension work, guided practice, and written production. Students taking lessons with us receive instant feedback on questions and personalized feedback, corrections, and guidance from their teacher on their written work. Teachers also tailor and assign specific portal activities based on what each student is working on in class, their individual needs, and the areas that would benefit from extra practice.

These nine materials, ranging from A1 to C2 (from elementary to advanced), are a small sample of what is available in our materials portal. Students enrolled in our lessons have access to the full portal, where they can explore additional materials at their own pace, review topics covered in class, revisit areas they find challenging, and continue studying beyond lesson time.

The materials on this page are open to explore—no username or password is required—so you can get a feel for the kind of support, practice, and learning resources available to our students before enrolling.
